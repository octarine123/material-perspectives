# List of ideas and research questions

## Mineral Supply, Production & Geology
How does mineral producer diversity correlate with long‑term supply resilience across critical mineral classes?

What geological factors most strongly predict whether a country transitions from raw mineral producer to refined‑material exporter?

How do land area and geological endowment interact to determine a country’s mineral production potential?

What is the relationship between mineral producer concentration and global price volatility for critical minerals?

How do mineral discovery rates differ between high‑income and low‑income countries, and what drives the gap?

## Manufacturing, Industrial Capability & Value Chains

How does manufacturer diversity influence a country’s ability to capture value from global mineral supply chains?

What macro‑economic conditions enable mineral‑rich countries to develop downstream manufacturing sectors?

How does industrial concentration in a small set of countries affect global technology diffusion?

What is the minimum sector diversity required for a country to become a competitive manufacturer of critical technologies?

How do producer–manufacturer gaps shape global dependency patterns in clean‑energy supply chains?

## acro‑Economics & Development
How does inequality (GNI coefficient) constrain industrial upgrading in mineral‑rich economies?

What is the relationship between GDP scale and a country’s ability to buffer mineral supply shocks?

How do population size and demographic structure influence mineral demand trajectories?

How does land area correlate with the likelihood of developing large‑scale extractive industries?

What macro‑economic indicators best predict successful mineral‑to‑manufacturing transitions?

## Geopolitics & Strategic Capability
How do mineral producer–manufacturer asymmetries shape geopolitical leverage in critical mineral markets?

What strategic capability score best predicts a country’s influence over future clean‑tech supply chains?

How do alliances and trade blocs mitigate mineral supply risk for manufacturing‑heavy economies?

How does resource nationalism affect global mineral availability and price stability?

What geopolitical risks emerge when mineral production is concentrated in politically unstable regions?

## Energy Transition & Technology Systems
How do mineral supply constraints affect the deployment speed of renewable energy technologies?

What minerals represent the highest systemic risk to global electrification pathways?

How does industrial capability influence a country’s ability to adopt next‑generation battery technologies?

What mineral bottlenecks most threaten global net‑zero targets?

How do mineral recycling rates alter long‑term supply–demand balance?

## Structural Asymmetry & Global Inequality
How does mineral wealth reinforce or reduce inequality within producer countries?

What structural factors prevent mineral‑rich countries from capturing manufacturing value?

How do mineral‑poor but manufacturing‑rich countries maintain dominance in high‑tech sectors?

How does inequality shape the distribution of benefits from global mineral supply chains?

What policy interventions most effectively reduce producer–manufacturer asymmetry?
