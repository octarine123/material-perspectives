# Outputs and publication assets

This directory contains publication graphics, charts, and branded visual output for Material Perspectives.

Files here are intended for attribution and research communication. They are not a general-purpose open asset library.

## Watermarking

Charts intended for Substack and publication use may include a Material Perspectives watermark or brand mark. This is a design choice to reinforce attribution and brand recognition, not a substitute for licensing terms.

Please keep the watermark and attribution visible when reusing publication graphics in external articles, newsletters, or presentations.

## Reuse guidance

- Attribution is required.
- Non-commercial reuse is permitted under CC BY-NC 4.0.
- Commercial use requires separate permission.
- Do not remove the watermark unless you are explicitly authorized to do so.
